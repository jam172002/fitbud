Delete your FitBud account

Sign in to permanently delete your FitBud account and its associated data.

What this does

  • Removes your profile, photos, and location/address data.
  • Deletes your buddy requests, friendships, and group memberships.
  • Redacts messages you sent so shared conversations aren't broken for the other person, and removes your own conversation history.
  • Deletes your gym check-in history and session invites.
  • Signs you out everywhere and deletes your login credentials.

What's kept, and why

Payment/order records tied to a subscription purchase are retained for financial record-keeping rather than deleted with the rest of your account. No other personal profile data is kept once deletion completes.

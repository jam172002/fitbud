FitBud Privacy Policy

This is the public version of the same policy shown in-app under Profile > Privacy & Security. It reflects how the FitBud app and backend actually behave.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, profile photo, and fitness preferences.
  • Fitness data: workout sessions, gym check-ins, exercise preferences, fitness goals, and activity history.
  • Social data: buddy connections, chat messages, session invites, and buddy requests.
  • Device information: device type, OS, app version, and push notification tokens.
  • Usage data: features you interact with and how you navigate the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve FitBud, including buddy matching.
  • Enable in-app chat between you and your fitness buddies.
  • Send push notifications about buddy requests, session invites, and messages, with your permission.
  • Process subscription payments and manage your premium plan status.
  • Review reports of abusive content or behavior and act on them under our Terms & Conditions.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With other users, as your public profile (name, photo, fitness preferences) when you appear in buddy searches or matches.
  • With service providers - we use Firebase (Google) for authentication, database, storage, and push notifications.
  • For legal reasons, or to protect the rights and safety of FitBud and its users.
  • In a business transfer, such as an acquisition or merger.

4. Data Retention

  • Profile, chat, and social data is kept until you delete your account. On deletion, your profile is de-identified and wholly personal subcollections (settings, addresses, notifications) are permanently removed.
  • Gym check-in history is automatically deleted after 24 months.
  • Payment/order records are retained after account deletion for financial record-keeping, separate from the rest of your profile.

5. Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS. Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication. Access to production data is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis. No system is completely secure - if you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

6. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access & correction of your profile information from within the app.
  • Notification preferences, from your device settings or the in-app Settings screen.
  • Account deletion - delete your account and its data from Settings > Delete Account in the app, or from this website without signing into the app. Deletion requires you to confirm your identity and cannot be undone.
  • Block & report - block a user to stop them from messaging or matching with you, and report content that violates our Terms & Conditions.
  • Data portability - request a copy of your personal data by contacting us below.

7. Children's Privacy

FitBud is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. Material changes will be announced in-app. Continued use of FitBud after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or how we handle your data: privacy@fitbud.app.
